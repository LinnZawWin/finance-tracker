Bulk entry

One transaction per line, in either form:
Description Amount [A|AA] [Account], for example Grocery Asian 113.56 A Flex
Date | Description | Merchant | Amount | Share flag | Account | Project or Notes (tab or | between fields). The last field is the project when it matches one, otherwise it goes to Notes.
A negative amount is saved as Income.
A line with only a date (19 Nov, 19/11, 2026-11-19) sets the date for the lines below it. Without a year it is this year, or last year for a month after this one. Share flag: blank = Mine, A = shared, AA = all for Partner. Rows of a day are saved 10 minutes apart, top line first. Rows pasted from the Keep Inbox sheet work as they are.
